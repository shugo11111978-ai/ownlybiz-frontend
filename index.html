Frequently asked questions

What is the platform fee and when is it charged? ↓
The platform fee is a percentage deducted automatically from each session payment. Starter experts pay 12%, Pro pays 8%, Scale pays 5%. The fee is shown transparently in your dashboard — you always see exactly what you earned and what Ownlybiz kept.
How do I get paid? ↓
Connect your own Stripe Connect, PayPal Business, or Coinbase Commerce account. Client payments flow directly to your payment account — Ownlybiz deducts the platform fee from each transaction. You receive payouts on your normal Stripe/PayPal schedule.
Can I use my own domain? ↓
Yes. You get a free subdomain (yourname.ownlybiz.com) instantly. On Pro and Scale plans, connect any custom domain — we handle DNS, SSL, and routing. Takes about 5 minutes in your dashboard.
Am I an employee of Ownlybiz? ↓
No. You are an independent professional using Ownlybiz's technology platform. Ownlybiz is not your employer, franchisor, or staffing agency. All services are provided by you, to your clients, under your own responsibility.
What happens after the 2-month free trial? ↓
After 2 months, Starter experts continue free with the 12% platform fee. Pro and Scale plans require a monthly or annual subscription. We'll give you 30 days notice before any charges — no surprises.
